Safe, patient-centred care for every family

The Ga Central Municipal Health Directorate delivers care supported by skilled clinicians, modern technology and a compassionate team. We treat every patient with dignity, and we measure our success by the health and trust of the families we serve.

Our mission

To provide accessible, high-quality healthcare that improves lives and strengthens our community.

Our vision

To be the most trusted healthcare partner in Greater Accra, recognised for clinical outcomes and patient experience.

Our values

Compassion, integrity, excellence, teamwork and respect for every patient and colleague.

Departments and services

Emergency and Trauma

24-hour emergency care with rapid triage, resuscitation and trauma response.

Cardiology

Diagnosis and treatment of heart conditions, including ECG, echo and cardiac rehabilitation.

Orthopaedics

Joint, bone and spine care, sports injuries and rehabilitation.

Maternity and Neonatal

Antenatal care, delivery suites and a neonatal intensive care unit.

Paediatrics

Child-friendly care from newborn to adolescence, including vaccinations.

Oncology

Multidisciplinary cancer care including chemotherapy and supportive services.

Neurology and Neurosurgery

Care for stroke, epilepsy, headache and complex neurological disorders.

General Surgery

Elective and emergency procedures, including minimally invasive surgery.

Internal Medicine

Comprehensive adult care and chronic disease management.

Radiology and Imaging

X-ray, ultrasound, CT and MRI with rapid reporting.

Laboratory Services

Accredited pathology and diagnostic testing around the clock.

Critical Care (ICU)

Advanced monitoring and life support for critically ill patients.
